Bounds of r by kernel (rBoundsByKernel, rRateBounds): legacy against new bounds
================================================================================
Made with r_bounds_by_kernel.py and r_bounds_by_kernel_summary.py (this folder),
Python port, Poisson likelihood (method1 = 1), 10 start points, seed 1.
"legacy" = rBoundsByKernel False: r from mean(first two counts)/200 for every kernel.
"new"    = default: logistic and Gompertz r in [1e-3, 10] per time step,
           Richards r from 1e-3 to the count-based upper bound.
GGM, GLM and GRM have the same bounds in both, so their fits do not change.

Windows (44): US COVID-19 deaths, 4 columns (USA and the 3 largest states) x 8
90-day windows ending on day 150, 230, ..., 710; weekly mpox USA, 5 windows;
Ebola Uganda and SARS Singapore, 3 windows each; yearly HIV, 1 window.
Forecast MAE: mean absolute error of the best-fit curve over the next 14 days
(4 weeks for mpox, 10 days for Ebola and SARS, 3 years for HIV), raw counts.
"K<0.5*cum": the fitted carrying capacities add up to less than half of the
cases already observed in the window (the fit ran away to a huge r).
AICc "same": within 2 units.
